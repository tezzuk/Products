# Brief 002 — Local Business Cold Outreach
*Assigned to: Agent 3 | Priority: HIGH | Day: 2*

## Objective
Send 20+ WhatsApp messages to local businesses offering a website/landing page. Close 3–5 clients at ₹2,000–₹5,000 each.

## Deliverables (Agent 3 to build)
1. `whatsapp_cold_message.md` — copy-paste WhatsApp message template
2. `follow_up_messages.md` — Day 2 and Day 4 follow-up scripts
3. `pricing_sheet.md` — simple offer card (can be sent as image)
4. `client_tracker.csv` — spreadsheet to track leads and status

## Target Businesses
- Local tutors / coaching centers
- Freelance photographers
- Home bakers / food businesses
- Yoga instructors / fitness trainers
- CA / lawyer offices without websites
- Local shops wanting online presence

## Offer
"I'll build you a professional 1-page website in 48 hours for just ₹2,000. Includes contact form + Google Maps. Limited spots this week."

## Agent 3 Actions
- [ ] Write the WhatsApp cold message (short, punchy, local language option)
- [ ] Build a sample landing page as proof of work
- [ ] Create follow-up sequence (2 messages, 2 days apart)
- [ ] Create a simple pricing image/PDF to share
