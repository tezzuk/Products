# Social Media Content Service Kit
*Agent 3 Deliverable | iPad-friendly business*

---

## The Offer

**Target:** Local small businesses with Instagram but inconsistent posting
(home bakers, tutors, coaches, salons, food stalls, boutiques)

**What you sell:** "I'll write and design 15 Instagram posts for your business for ₹1,500"

---

## Cold Message (WhatsApp — send to local business owners you know or find on Instagram)

```
Hi [Name]! 👋

I noticed your Instagram page for [Business Name] — your work looks great!

I help small businesses like yours stay active on Instagram with consistent, 
professional posts — without you having to spend hours writing captions.

I'll create 15 ready-to-post content pieces (captions + design briefs) for ₹1,500.
You just post them — takes 2 minutes a day.

Interested? Happy to share a sample first. 😊
```

---

## How to Deliver (Claude does everything)

**Step 1:** Client tells you their business, what they sell, their vibe

**Step 2:** Use this Claude prompt:
```
Create 15 Instagram post ideas for a [type of business] called [name].
Their target audience is [audience]. Their tone is [fun/professional/warm].
They want to promote: [products/services].

For each post give:
1. Post type (tip, behind the scenes, product showcase, testimonial, etc.)
2. Caption (with emojis, hashtags, CTA)
3. Visual description (what the image/reel should show)
```

**Step 3:** Format into a Google Doc, share link with client

**Step 4:** Offer to do it monthly for ₹2,000/month (recurring income!)

## Finding Clients via iPad

1. Search Instagram for local businesses in your city with <1000 followers
2. Check if they post inconsistently (last post was weeks ago)
3. DM them the cold message above
4. Send 20 DMs/day — expect 2–3 responses, 1 close

**20 DMs/day × 7 days = 140 outreach = 7–14 clients = ₹10,500–₹21,000**
