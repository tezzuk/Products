# Tool 2: WhatsApp Fee Reminder Message Templates

**For:** Coaching Center Owners
**Language:** Hindi + English mix (natural, conversational)
**Usage:** Copy the message, fill in the placeholders [ ], send to parent

---

## HOW TO USE THESE TEMPLATES

1. Copy the message
2. Replace all `[PLACEHOLDERS]` with actual information
3. Send via WhatsApp to the parent's number

**Placeholders used:**
- `[Student Name]` — e.g., Rahul
- `[Parent Name]` — e.g., Rajesh Ji
- `[Amount]` — e.g., ₹1,500
- `[Due Date]` — e.g., 5 June
- `[Month]` — e.g., June
- `[Center Name]` — e.g., Sharma Classes
- `[Days]` — number of days overdue
- `[Receipt No]` — payment receipt number (optional)

---

## TEMPLATE 1 — Monthly Fee Due Reminder (Friendly)

*Use: 2–3 days before the due date*

---

Namaste [Parent Name] 🙏

[Center Name] ki taraf se aapko yaad dilana chahte hain ki [Student Name] ki **[Month] month ki fees — ₹[Amount]** ki due date **[Due Date]** hai.

Fees jama karne ke liye aap hamare centre par aa sakte hain ya UPI ke zariye bhi payment kar sakte hain.

Koi bhi samasya ho to zaroor batayein. Dhanyavaad! 😊

— [Center Name] Team

---

*English version (optional, if parent prefers):*

Hello [Parent Name],

This is a friendly reminder that [Student Name]'s fees for **[Month] — ₹[Amount]** are due on **[Due Date]**.

You can pay at the centre or via UPI. Please let us know if you have any questions.

Thank you!
— [Center Name]

*Use: When payment is 15 or more days late*

[Student Name] ki **[Month] ki fees — ₹[Amount]** ko **[Days] din** se zyada ho gaye hain aur abhi tak payment receive nahi hui.

Agar aap koi arrangement chahte hain, hum samajhenge aur milke kuch solution nikalenge.

## PRO TIPS FOR THE OWNER

- Always address the parent by name — it feels personal, not automated
- Send reminders between 9 AM–12 PM or 5 PM–7 PM — best response times
- Never send after 9 PM — it feels rude
- If a parent replies with difficulty paying, always respond kindly and offer to discuss in person
- Save these templates in your phone's "Notes" app for quick access
