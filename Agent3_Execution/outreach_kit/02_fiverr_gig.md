## FAQ SECTION

**Q: What file format do you need?**
A: Any Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file works. As long as it has a Date column, a Product/Item column, and a Revenue/Amount column, I can work with it. Other formats can also work — just message me first.

**Q: How much data do I need?**
A: Minimum 1 month, ideally 3-6 months. More data = better forecast accuracy.

**Q: I use Tally/Busy/other billing software. Can I still use this?**
A: Yes. Export your sales register to Excel from any billing software and send that file.

**Q: What will the report look like?**
A: A PDF document with charts and a written summary. Check my gig images for a sample.

**Q: Do you keep my data confidential?**
A: Yes. Your data is used only to generate your report and deleted after delivery.

**Q: Can you handle restaurant data with tips, tables, etc.?**
A: Yes, I regularly work with restaurant sales data including item-wise breakdowns.
