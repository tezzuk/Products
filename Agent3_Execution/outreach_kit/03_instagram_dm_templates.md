# Instagram DM Templates — Cold Outreach to Local Shops

---

## HOW TO FIND TARGETS

**Search Instagram for:**
- `#kanpurfood` `#kanpurfoodie` `#kanpurrestaurant`
- `#kanpurshopping` `#kanpurfashion` `#kanpurboutique`
- `#kanpursweets` `#kanpurcafe` `#kanpurstore`
- Any shop with a local bio like "📍 Kanpur | DM for orders"

**Signs of a good target:**
- Has 200–5000 followers (small enough to not have analytics tools, big enough to care)
- Posts product photos or daily specials
- Has a WhatsApp number in bio (means they're open to business DMs)
- Responds to comments (active owner)

**Volume goal: 50 DMs per day. Takes ~30 minutes.**

---

## DM TEMPLATE 1 — Restaurant / Cafe

```
Hi! 👋

Love what you're doing with [mention something specific — "the thali photos" / "the daily specials" / "the vibe of your place"].

Quick question — do you track which dishes sell best on which days, or which hours bring the most customers?

I build simple sales reports for restaurants — you send me your billing data, I give you back a clear breakdown of what's working. Takes me 24 hours.

Would a free sample report on your data be useful to see?
```

```

```

If you're interested in one for your shop, just reply and I'll explain the process. Takes 10 minutes on your end, 24 hours on mine.

## COMMENT STRATEGY (works alongside DMs)

On posts by target shops, leave a genuine comment first before DMing:
- "This looks amazing 🔥" on a food post
- "Love the display!" on a product photo
- "Great collection this season"

Wait 1 day → then DM. They'll recognize the name → higher open rate.
