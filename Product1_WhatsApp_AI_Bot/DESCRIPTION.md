# Product 1 — AI WhatsApp Business Bot
## What It Does
A 24/7 AI assistant that answers customer WhatsApp messages for any business —
menu queries, timings, appointment requests, pricing — automatically.
When the bot doesn't know something, it collects the customer's name and phone,
and the owner gets a neat "callback needed" list.

## Who To Pitch
- **Restaurants & Dhabas** (Civil Lines, Naveen Market) — answer menu/timing questions all day
- **Dental & Medical Clinics** (Swaroop Nagar, Arya Nagar) — appointment booking queries
- **Coaching Centers** (Kalyanpur, Kakadeo) — fee/batch/admission queries from parents
- Anyone who says "I spend 2 hours/day answering the same WhatsApp questions"

## Pricing
₹8,000–₹12,000 one-time setup
₹500/month optional maintenance

## Demo Instructions
1. Open the app
2. Fill in the client's business details on the left (takes 2 min)
3. Press "Start Demo Chat"
4. Type messages as if you're a customer: "Hello", "Menu bhejo", "Timings kya hain?"
5. Show them the leads panel — a real customer inquiry was just "collected"
6. That's the moment they're sold
