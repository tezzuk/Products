# Product 2 — Business Intelligence Dashboard
## What It Does
A live web dashboard that answers the 3 questions every business owner asks daily:
1. How much money did I make this month?
2. Which products/services are selling best?
3. Who are my top customers?

Business owner uploads their sales data (Excel/CSV) or enters it manually.
Dashboard shows charts, trends, and instant insights. Live URL they can bookmark.

## Who To Pitch
- **Leather manufacturers & exporters** — track which SKUs sell on Amazon/Flipkart/export
- **Textile shops** (Naveen Market, Birhana Road) — which fabric moves, which doesn't
- **Medical stores** — daily revenue, which medicines expire vs. sell
- **Restaurants** — after selling the WhatsApp bot, upsell this: "Now let's see which dishes sell most"

**Best used as an UPSELL** after closing Product 1 or 3.

## Demo Instructions
1. Open the app — it loads with sample restaurant data immediately
2. Show the metrics at top: Total Revenue, Orders, Avg Order Value
3. Show the revenue trend chart — point at peaks and dips
4. Switch Business Type dropdown to match their industry
5. Say: "When I set this up for you, this is YOUR data here — not sample."
6. Show the CSV upload: "You export from wherever you track sales, upload here."
