# Product 4 — Competitor Price Monitor
## What It Does
Automatically checks competitor prices on Amazon/Flipkart every morning
and sends a WhatsApp alert when:
- A competitor drops their price (you need to respond)
- A competitor goes out of stock (opportunity to raise your price)
- Your product's rank changes

Business owner wakes up, WhatsApp alert is already there. No manual checking.

## Who To Pitch
- **Amazon/Flipkart leather goods sellers** (Kanpur = leather capital of India)
  Search "leather wallet" on Amazon → many sellers are from Kanpur/UP
- **Textile sellers on Meesho/Flipkart** (Naveen Market, Birhana Road)
- Anyone selling 5+ products on any e-commerce platform

## How To Find Them
1. Go to amazon.in → search "leather wallet India"
2. Click any product → click seller name → "Sold by: XYZ"  
3. Many sellers list their IndiaMART/TradeIndia page in their profile
4. Search "leather bag manufacturer Kanpur" on IndiaMART → get phone numbers

Push for the monthly plan — 5 clients × ₹1,000 = ₹5,000/month passive even after the 2 weeks.
